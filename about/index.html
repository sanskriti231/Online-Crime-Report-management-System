About the project


The Suraksha Saathi Bureau is a web-based platform for modernizing and streamlining processes involving filling, tracking, and management of crime reports. This system provides a smarter alternative for traditional ways of complaint lodging and crime-reporting procedures so that users may register their complaints and report crimes as well as track cases from their home.


It addresses the drawbacks of manual processes in terms of delays, poor record management, and restricted access. In using technology, it ensures improved transparency, faster processing, and better communication between citizens and law enforcement authorities.


Key features of the system include:

  • User-Friendly Interface: Easy-to-use portals for citizens and administrators.
  • Users Crime Report Submission: Users can submit detailed crime reports online with necessary attachments.
  • Case Tracking: Complainants can track the status of their cases in real time.
  • Secure Database Management: Ensures safe storage of sensitive information with restricted access.
  • Analytics and Reporting: Law enforcement can generate insightful reports for better crime analysis and prevention.
